## Interpretation boundary

The 99.99% figure measures reusable mapping-node content objects in the synthetic append case. KV Archive still downloads/receives and hashes the latest full raw graph for an already-detected changed conversation. `incrementalBytesReused` estimates avoided payload writes for the current cycle and is not an exact lifetime disk-space counter.

## Behavioral guarantees

- dry-run performs no writes;
- package identity is derived from deterministic payload content;
- imports are all-or-nothing when conflicts exist;
- safe fast-forward requires exact lineage;
- recovery receipts are append-only;
- rollback never rewrites Raw Evidence or approved Project State;
- later local work blocks destructive rollback;
- unbound exports do not include Project-bound content.
